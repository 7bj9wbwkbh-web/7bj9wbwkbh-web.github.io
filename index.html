Home Integration

A private, personal home-automation integration for a single household.

What this is

Home Integration reads status from the owner's own Google Nest devices (cameras, doorbell, thermostat, and display) through Google's Device Access and Smart Device Management API.

It is not a commercial product. There are no public sign-ups, no accounts for other people, and no service offered to the public. Only the owner uses it, for devices in that one household.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes the Nest device data the integration accesses, how that data is used, and how access can be revoked.
